Appendix H1 The triangle model

Igor Makowski   M1

Part a Matrix form

The objective is min 8y1 + 7y2 + 7y3.

c = (8,7,7)^T and b = (1,1,1)^T

A has rows (1,1,0) and (0,1,1) and (1,0,1).

The relaxation is min c^T y with Ay >= b and 0 <= y <= 1. There are three variables and three structural rows. Bounds are not counted as rows.

Part b Changing the row senses

Multiplying each row by -1 gives -Ay <= -b. The rows are -y1 - y2 <= -1 and -y2 - y3 <= -1 and -y1 - y3 <= -1. For minimization the original >= prices are nonnegative. After the change each price is negated and becomes nonpositive. Here pi = (4,3,4) becomes (-4,-3,-4). The product of each price and its row remains unchanged.

Part c All eight binary points
